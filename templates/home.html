Key Innovation

OpenCV Brain Contour Cropping

Before any pixel enters the neural network, our preprocessing pipeline uses OpenCV to detect the brain's boundaries and crop away black borders — dramatically improving model focus and accuracy.

🔲
1. Grayscale + Blur

Convert to grayscale, apply Gaussian blur (5×5) to reduce noise before thresholding.

🧩
2. Contour Detection

Binary threshold at pixel 45 + erode/dilate, then find the largest external contour — the brain outline.

✂️
3. Extreme-Point Crop

Use the 4 extreme contour points (left, right, top, bottom) to crop a tight bounding box around the brain.
